公司简介 Company Introduction

Shaoxing Xunchi
Textiles Co., Ltd

Shaoxing Xunchi Textiles Co., Ltd is a home textiles manufacturer founded in 2010, specialized in various knit fabrics and blankets (fleece, polar fleece, coral fleece, flannel, picnic, and double-sided blankets, etc.), cushions, bedding, and other home textile products.

Also, our products have OEKO-TEX Standard 100. The factory located in Shaoxing City, Zhejiang Province, China, which is near Ningbo Port and Shanghai Port. Our factory is audited by BSCI. With more than 14 years of development, we have our own fabric weaving workshop, dyeing workshop and ready-made workshop. Warmly welcome you to audit our factory.

FAQ

What do you manufacture?

Xunchi focuses on blankets and home textiles, including flannel blankets, sherpa blankets, polar fleece blankets, coral fleece blankets, double-ply blankets, printable white blankets for DIY and custom printing, picnic mats, beach towels, bath towels, cushions, shawls, face masks, and other related home textile products.

Do you handle custom orders?

Yes. The site positions Xunchi as a custom manufacturing supplier for buyers who need custom products, labels, packaging, and design support.

Why is the location important?

The factory is in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, close to Ningbo Port and Shanghai Port, which strengthens export logistics and is useful for international sourcing teams.
